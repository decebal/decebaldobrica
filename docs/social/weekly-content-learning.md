# Learning from weekly content reports

Use for Decebal's performance analysis and future LinkedIn posts or connected X threads in either series. Read the latest available report and campaign evidence before selecting a hypothesis.

## Interpret the evidence

Keep account totals, scheduler-post totals and individual-post counts separate. Record provider, report window, capture date, metric definitions and post URL or identifiable excerpt. Mark ambiguous reach labels unresolved. Preserve conflicting labels and values; do not equate impressions with unique members reached.

Reconcile row sums with the scheduler summary before comparing reports. Investigate definitions and capture timing without inventing explanations. Record missing metrics as unavailable, never zero. Preserve reported zeros, disclose archive gaps and keep Buffer multi-channel summaries separate from Supergrow LinkedIn rows.

Compare posts at equal publication ages and separate full posts, morning minutes and leadership posts. Keep raw counts beside rates and their denominators. Flag outliers and repeated variants. Comment totals may include the author; separate other people's comments when available. Treat report advice as hypotheses, not causal evidence. Reactions establish neither reader retention nor business conversion. Account-wide follower growth cannot establish post attribution.

## Write the next post

Choose one engineering reader and one decision. Open with a concrete failure, decision or observable consequence. Put a usable action in the opening section, explain its mechanism and provide a short procedure or worked example.

Retain essential caveats, versions, source links and opposing arguments. Move detail after the action only when doing so preserves meaning. Check hooks as strictly as bodies. Reject invented personal experience and unsupported numbers. Historical popularity does not verify a claim: check model existence, release status and performance before reuse. Label hypothetical cases explicitly.

Recall current prime_voice preferences before first-person writing. Skill examples are not biography. Use SLAY or PAS when it fits, ordinary British prose and readable paragraphs. Avoid forced contrarianism, repeated binary contrasts, rhetorical reveals, decorative Unicode and generic agreement prompts. A useful question asks about one decision or observable failure. End with a concrete action when no honest question adds value. Never manufacture disagreement for comments.

## Learn without changing the calendar

Preserve the authorised cadence and occupied slots. Contradictory weekday advice cannot justify rescheduling. Do not infer an X strategy from LinkedIn-only evidence. Keep threads connected and preserve root artwork, alt text and sources.

Change one editorial variable per comparison. Test an earlier action within comparable posts and the established calendar. Record hypothesis, affected idea IDs, original and revised copy, source caveats, review outcome and measurement plan. Offline scores are proxies, not measured reach. Retain revisions only after evidence checks and no-ai-slop review. Recheck time-sensitive claims immediately before a platform write. Evaluate published outcomes before declaring improvement.
