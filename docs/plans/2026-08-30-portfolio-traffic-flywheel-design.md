# Portfolio traffic flywheel

Status: approved for implementation on 30 August 2026.

## Goal

Turn two owned authority sites into clear discovery routes for eight focused
products, then measure whether visitors reach useful product evidence and
complete each bet's real activation action.

## System

1. `wolventech.com/products` is the canonical company product directory.
2. `decebaldobrica.com/products` explains founder context and links to the same
   product evidence without duplicating product claims.
3. Every product has one flagship proof page answering one high-intent question.
4. Directory links carry stable UTM and `bet` values. Product domains remain the
   canonical destinations.
5. Search, AI answers, communities, creator demonstrations, and Play listings
   distribute the proof page rather than generic homepages.
6. Paid acquisition remains blocked until a tagged organic source produces the
   bet's qualified completion or purchase.

## Measurement contract

Owned directory links use:

- `utm_source`: `wolventech.com` or `decebaldobrica.com`
- `utm_medium`: `portfolio`
- `utm_campaign`: `product_directory`
- `utm_content`: product slug plus `product` or `proof`
- `bet`: stable product slug

Weekly review uses Search Console impressions, clicks, query/page CTR; product
analytics source-tagged activation; and Play listing visits, acquisition, first
open, and activation. Unknown stays unknown. Internal QA never counts as demand.

## Guardrails

- No sitewide cross-link web between every product.
- One relevant company/founder directory link per product is enough.
- No scaled generic articles, invented testimonials, or unproved outcome claims.
- No paid traffic while delivery, value-proof, refund, submission, or production
  blockers remain.
- Product `BET.md` remains source of truth for audience, claim, price, and gate.
