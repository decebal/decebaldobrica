## Design Context

### Users

- `decebaldobrica.com` serves technical founders and CTOs evaluating Decebal as an engineering leader, systems architect, or hands-on partner.
- `wolventech.com` serves Rust platform leaders, Seed-to-Series-B teams, VC partners, and acquirers buying delivery, architecture, or technical diligence.
- Both audiences need fast proof of judgment, delivery discipline, and safe AI-assisted engineering—not broad claims about AI productivity.

### Brand Personality

- Decebal Dobrica: rigorous, candid, evidence-led.
- Wolven Tech: industrial, calm, exact.
- Voice favors concrete constraints, public artifacts, measured outcomes, and clear ownership.
- Avoid hype, agent theatre, inflated transformation language, and generic consultancy copy.

### Aesthetic Direction

- Preserve each site's established identity: teal/navy editorial portfolio for Decebal; rust/amber utilitarian systems aesthetic for Wolven Tech.
- Use workflow diagrams, gates, manifests, evidence trails, and repository artifacts when they explain real operating practice.
- Avoid generic card walls, interchangeable SaaS layouts, excessive pills, AI gradients, glowing purple effects, and decorative terminal chrome without meaning.
- Motion stays restrained and purposeful. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Design Principles

1. Show method through evidence. Connect every operating claim to a gate, artifact, repository, or outcome.
2. Adapt framing by buyer. Personal site explains collaboration and judgment; Wolven Tech explains delivery controls and risk reduction.
3. Make scanning decisive. Strong hierarchy, short proof-led copy, and one clear next action per section.
4. Preserve accessibility. Semantic structure, keyboard operation, visible focus, WCAG AA contrast, and useful text without animation.
5. Prefer owned proof. Link public source, releases, checks, and documentation instead of relying on logos or unsupported metrics.
